# Procurement notice review

Review procurement notices against the supplied review items and record judgments with supporting evidence.

## Language

**Procurement notice (공고)**:
One notice, comprising its notice text, attached-document text and registration metadata.

**Review item (검토 항목)**:
A supplied criterion against which a procurement notice is assessed.

**Item group (항목 그룹)**:
A set of review items assessed together for one procurement notice.

**Judgment conversation (판정 대화)**:
The dialogue assessing one item group for one procurement notice. Its search history and judgments belong to that dialogue, independently of other item groups and notices.

**Judgment (판정)**:
The violation decision and associated evidence for one review item in one procurement notice.

**Evidence (근거문구)**:
Supporting text quoted from a procurement notice's original documents. Retrieved legal material informs the judgment but is not evidence quoted from the notice.
